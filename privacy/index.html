Privacy Policy

What We Collect

If you enter for free, we collect your name, email address, and state of residence through our entry form. If you enter by buying socks, your order — including your email address — is processed by Square; we receive the order and email address needed to record your entry, but your payment details are handled entirely by Square and never touch our systems.

How We Use It

We use this information to run the Sweepstakes, verify eligibility, contact the winner, and fulfill sock orders. If you opt in, we'll also send you Don't Spike emails — you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information.

Questions

Contact us at info@dontspike.com about your entry, your order, or this policy.
