Privacy Policy

Don't Spike collects only the information needed to run the Sweepstakes: name, email address, and state of residence for free entries, and the email address on a sock order for purchase entries. We use it to administer the Sweepstakes, contact the winner, and, if you opt in, to send Don't Spike emails. We do not sell your information. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email, or contact info@dontspike.com about your entry.
